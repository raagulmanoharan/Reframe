Your own little photo studio.

Describe any scene and optionally add a product reference. AI turns it into a rough 3D composition. Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to move closer, and use two fingers or right-drag to pan.

Capture your favourite angles, then select Generate photos. Each selected composition and its reference are sent to the AI image model. Tap a finished photo to view it. Download saves your selected photos or composition drafts.

AI approximates the reference in 3D; it is not an exact reconstruction. Review final images for product accuracy. Images use the model’s supported output sizes, so aspect ratios may vary slightly. Shots clear when you reload.

Prompts, references, and captures are sent directly to OpenAI when you generate. Your API key stays in tab memory and is cleared on reload. This app has no backend and does not store your images or key.
